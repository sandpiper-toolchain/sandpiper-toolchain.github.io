## Sandpiper toolchain talk

We are fresh back from a fantastic week of science and community at the EGU General Assembly 2026 in Vienna! 

During the meeting, Andrew Moodie gave an invited talk highlighting the latest developments within the **sandpiper** toolchain. 
The presentation focused on our ongoing work to build open, robust software tools that streamline data sharing and analysis pipelines across the geomorphology community. 

A big thank you to everyone who stopped by the session, asked questions, and shared ideas for future features and collaborations!

* **Check out the abstract:** You can view the formal session details and abstract via the [EGU Sphere presentation link](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-egu26-11609).
* **Get involved:** Head over to our [GitHub organization page](https://github.com/sandpiper-toolchain) to explore code, report issues, or contribute to the next wave of developments.
